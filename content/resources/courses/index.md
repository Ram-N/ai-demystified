# Gen AI Courses around the web

Welcome to the "AI Demystified" resources hub! This section is designed to help you continue your learning journey in Artificial Intelligence. Whether you prefer reading, watching, or hands-on practice, you'll find a curated list of beginner-friendly resources to deepen your understanding of AI concepts and applications.

## Introductory AI and GenAI Courses

- **AI for Beginners (Microsoft)**
  - 18-video playlist covering everything from LLMs, prompt engineering, applications, and agents to open source models.
  - [Watch the full course on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k7HaeJs-N-o) (4h 20m)

- **Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (IBM/Coursera)**
  - Covers AI/GenAI basics, neural networks, LLMs, and practical applications. Ideal as a companion to lectures.
  - [Watch/Join Course on Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/learn/introduction-to-ai)

- **Elements of AI (University of Helsinki/MinnaLearn)**
  - Free online course, non-technical introduction, accessible for all. Over a million students enrolled.
  - [Visit Elements of AI](https://www.elementsofai.com)

- **Generative AI Full Course 2025 (Edureka)**
  - Hands-on learning: LLMs, GANs, prompt engineering, agents, ethics, AI in industry, and real projects.
  - [Watch the full course](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Ahl73azv7U) (9h+)

- **Google’s AI Course for Beginners (in 10 minutes)**
  - Quick essential overview with animated visuals—great for fast orientation.
  - [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yq0QkCxoTHM)


- **Generative AI Full Course 2025 | Gen AI Tutorial for Beginners (Simplilearn)**
  - Step-by-step GenAI concept explanations, LLMs to real-world applications.
  - [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QnzZNKueUmo)
