# Gen AI Blogs and Articles

Here are several blogs and articles that about "what GenAI really is"

1. **<a href="https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/generative-ai" target="_blank">What is Generative AI?</a>** – IBM  
IBM has done a remarkable job of giving an overview in one article. The embedded vides are well worth watching as quick intros to many of GenAI concepts.
<a href="https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/generative-ai" target="_blank">Read here </a>

2. **Explained: Generative AI** – MIT News  
Offers an accessible explanation of what generative AI is, including recent breakthroughs and key applications.  
<a href="https://news.mit.edu/2023/explained-generative-ai-1109" target="_blank">Read here</a>

3. **A Beginner’s Guide to Understanding Generative AI** – Jonathan Woahn  
Covers concepts like model “training,” “hallucination,” and “prompt engineering” in simple language for newcomers.  
<a href="https://blog.jonathanwoahn.com/a-beginners-guide-to-understanding-generative-ai-b2caceaead96" target="_blank">Read here</a>

4. **Generative AI Beginner’s Guide** – Lore  
A step-by-step educational guide covering the technical foundations, history, tools, ethical aspects, and real-world examples.  
<a href="https://lore.com/generative-ai" target="_blank">Read here</a>

5. **Getting Started with Generative AI: A Beginner’s Guide to Mastery** – HatchWorks  
A practical beginner’s guide focused on choosing platforms, setting up projects, best practices, and learning communities.  
<a href="https://hatchworks.com/blog/gen-ai/generative-ai-beginners/" target="_blank">Read here</a>

6. **Generative AI: Introduction to All Types of Gen-AI Models 2025** – Third Rock Techkno  
A thorough 2025 introduction to the various generative AI model types and their industry applications.  
<a href="https://www.thirdrocktechkno.com/blog/generative-ai-introduction-to-all-types-of-gen-ai-models-2025/" target="_blank">Read here</a>

7. **A Brief History of Generative AI** – DATAVERSITY  
Provides helpful context on how generative AI evolved—from early chatbots to modern foundation models.  
<a href="https://www.dataversity.net/a-brief-history-of-generative-ai/" target="_blank">Read here</a>

8. **Generative AI Beginner’s Guide** – Google Cloud  
Google’s official step-by-step guide outlining how generative AI fits into current technology and practical use cases.  
<a href="https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/learn/overview" target="_blank">Read here</a>

9. **What is ChatGPT, DALL-E, and generative AI?** – McKinsey Explainers  
Helps newcomers distinguish generative AI from other AI, with real-world examples (ChatGPT, DALL-E) and implications for business.  
<a href="https://www.mckinsey.com/featured-insights/mckinsey-explainers/what-is-generative-ai" target="_blank">Read here</a>

10. **Generative AI Roadmap 2025: Master NLP & Gen AI Step by Step** – Reddit /r/DeepLearning  
A Reddit community-curated roadmap of foundational knowledge and essential concepts, step by step.  
<a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/deeplearning/comments/1lyk360/generative_ai_roadmap_2025_master_nlp_gen_ai_step/" target="_blank">Read here</a>
