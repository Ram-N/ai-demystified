## Definition
Model parameters (theta) are the internal knobs and levers of the LLM. In a deep neural network, these are the weights and biases associated with the connections (arcs) and neurons (nodes) throughout the network. These numerical values give LLMs structure and behavior, enabling it to make predictions.
