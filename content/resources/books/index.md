# Beginner-Friendly Resources for Learning Generative AI

1. **[Generative AI for Beginners by Microsoft]**: An 18-lesson course introducing the fundamentals of generative AI applications.  
   [Link to Course](https://microsoft.com)

2. **[Elements of AI]**: A free online course covering the basics of artificial intelligence, including generative AI concepts.  
   [Link to Course](https://elementsofai.com)

3. **[Introduction to Generative AI and LLMs]**: The first part of a series exploring how generative AI and large language models work.  
   [Link to Article](https://example.com)

4. **[Getting Started with Generative AI: A Beginner's Guide to Mastery]**: Covers essentials of generative AI, including key concepts and practical applications.  
   [Link to Guide](https://example.com)

5. **[Beginner: Introduction to Generative AI Learning Path by Google Cloud Skills Boost]**: An overview of generative AI concepts, from the fundamentals of large language models to responsible AI principles.  
   [Link to Learning Path](https://cloud.google.com)
