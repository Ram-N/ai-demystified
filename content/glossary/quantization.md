## Definition
The process by which an AI large learning model is made smaller and more efficient (albeit, slightly less accurate) by lowering its precision from a higher format to a lower format. 

### Analogy for Quantization
A good way to think about this is to compare a 16-megapixel image to an 8-megapixel image. Both are still clear and visible, but the higher resolution image will have more detail when you're zoomed in.
