# Gen AI Blog to follow, keep an eye on

Here are several blogs and articles that about "what GenAI really is"
<a href="blogs_what_is_gen_ai.html" target="_blank">Find those here</a>

When it comes to authoritative blogs for understanding **generative AI fundamentals**, the following options are recognized leaders either due to deep technical expertise, direct involvement in foundational research, or an academic approach. These resources are widely trusted for clear explanations, conceptual depth, and up-to-date information:

## Authoritative Blogs for Generative AI Fundamentals

- **<a href="https://openai.com/news/" target="_blank">Open AI Blog</a>**
Frequently updated by the organization behind GPT models, OpenAI’s blog covers foundational concepts, technical breakthroughs, and responsible AI development, making it one of the most respected sources for generative AI insights.

- **<a href="https://bair.berkeley.edu/blog/" target="_blank">Berkeley Artificial Intelligence Research (BAIR) Blog</a>**  
BAIR is among the top academic AI research groups globally. Their blog features thorough, research-oriented posts on the principles and evolution of generative AI—especially useful for those wanting conceptual and mathematical depth.

- **<a href="https://blog.research.google/view/magazine" target="_blank">Google Research Blog / Google DeepMind Blog</a>**  
Google’s researchers are core contributors to many generative AI advances (e.g., transformers, diffusion models). Both blogs offer technical explanations, project spotlights, and conceptual guides directly from creators of the core technology.

- **<a href="https://machinelearningmastery.com/blog/" target="_blank">Machine Learning Mastery (by Jason Brownlee)</a>**  
Known for clear step-by-step tutorials, this blog excels in making foundational concepts (including GANs and LLMs) approachable for newcomers while retaining accuracy[1].

- **<a href="https://arbisoft.com/blogs/generative-ai-fundamentals-understanding-generative-models" target="_blank">Arbisoft: Generative AI Fundamentals</a>**
A specialized blog post breaking down the fundamentals of generative models, the role of transformer architectures, and the distinction between generative and traditional machine learning, with practical examples

- **<a href="https://nordcloud.com/blog/" target="_blank">Nordcloud: Generative AI 101</a>**  
Covers the basics of generative AI, foundational and large language models, and the differences from traditional models—with a focus on business use cases and core concepts

- **<a href="https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/generative-ai-for-everyone/" target="_blank">DeepLearning.AI Blog & Courses</a>**  
Andrew Ng’s DeepLearning.AI platform’s blog and learning offerings combine industry perspective and approachable technical explanations. Their “Generative AI for Everyone” reflects practical, foundational teaching on generative AI.

- **<a href="https://www.kdnuggets.com/news/index.html" target="_blank">KDnuggets & Towards AI</a>**  
Both are respected aggregators and editorial blogs in AI, regularly featuring detailed explainers on basics and developments in generative AI
